Privacy Policy

Last updated September 19, 2026

Information we collect

When you join the RISE waitlist, we collect the name and email address you provide, along with your waitlist number, confirmation code, signup time, and email delivery status.

How we use it

We use this information to manage the waitlist, send your confirmation, share meaningful product and launch updates, prevent abuse, and improve the signup experience.

Service providers

RISE uses Vercel to host the website, Supabase to store waitlist records, and Resend to deliver email. These providers process information only as needed to provide their services.

Cookies and analytics

The current waitlist does not intentionally set advertising cookies. Our hosting provider may process basic technical information, such as IP address and request logs, to operate and protect the service.

Your choices

You may ask to access, correct, or delete your waitlist information, or opt out of updates, by emailing rise.app00@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this policy as RISE develops. The date above will show the latest revision.
